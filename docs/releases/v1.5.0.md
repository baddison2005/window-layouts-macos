# Window Layouts Experimental v1.5.0

This release adds an opt-in Dock guard that helps keep the macOS Dock on your
preferred display without disabling **Displays have separate Spaces**.

## New: Experimental Dock guard

- Select the built-in MacBook display or another preferred monitor.
- Choose **Strong** protection to guard incoming mouse movement at other
  displays' exposed bottom edges, or **Gentle** protection for idle pointers.
- Cross shared display edges freely at any speed in either direction. The guard
  exempts sections connecting an upper external monitor to a MacBook below.
- Continue dragging and resizing with held mouse buttons; hold Option (or your
  chosen bypass modifier) to reach an exact exposed edge or deliberately move
  the Dock.
- Disable protection immediately from the menu bar, the app's Dock right-click
  menu, or Settings. Shutdown remains effective even if saving settings fails.
- Refresh display geometry on system notifications and check it every two
  seconds while active. Suspend when the preferred monitor disconnects, during
  sleep, when required permissions are unavailable, or with a side-positioned Dock.

## Setup and limitations

Position the Dock at the bottom of your preferred display, then open
**General → Experimental Dock guard**, enable protection, choose the display
and strength, and click **Apply**. The feature is disabled by default.

The guard uses public macOS APIs and requires Accessibility and input-event
permissions. It prevents accidental Dock activation rather than assigning the
Dock through a dedicated system API. Shared edges, held buttons, and bypass
modifiers are intentionally exempt. It does not automatically return an
already-moved Dock after wake or reconnection. The README includes instructions
for recovering stale input-event access after switching between Xcode and
installed builds.

## Downloads

The ZIP and DMG contain the same universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs
running macOS 14.6 or later. Both are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
Move **Window Layouts Experimental.app** to Applications and quit other copies
before launching it.

This is an official release on the experimental channel. It retains the
edition's separate identity and settings, including the Acrobat Space-movement
fix from v1.4.2. The stable edition has a separate update channel.
